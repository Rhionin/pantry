# Don't-restock mockups

Static screens for [#231](https://github.com/Rhionin/stockbot/issues/231). The application is unchanged.

Open `index.html?s=a-item` (and `a-shop`, `a-rules`, `a-review`, `b-item`, `b-shop`, `b-rules`, `b-review`). `./capture.sh` rebuilds the 390px and 1440px screenshots in `screenshots/`.

Two directions, same household story. The shopping screens are the moment you leave bananas off, before Produce is a rule. The item, settings, and review screens are later: Produce is left off, and lemons are the exception.

**In the row.** The flag rewrites a sentence on the item. Undo replaces the shopping row you just changed. Category rules are a section of Settings. Review is the inventory filter `Not restocked`.

**The slip.** One tally, titled Left off, is on the item, beside the shopping list, and is the category editor and the review list. Nothing routes away. On a phone the tally sits above the shopping rows, so Undo is on screen.

Stock colors stay about quantity. Full is teal, low is amber, out is red. "Left off" is a word. The amber wash is only the row that just changed.
