Privacy policy

Pantry is a personal project for one household. The server you run stores the inventory, the shopping cart, and, when you connect a Kroger account, the Kroger tokens for that account.

That data is not sold. It is not sent to a third party except Kroger, when you connect an account and write a cart.

A barcode you scan may be looked up in a public product database to find a name. That lookup does not include your Kroger account, and the name is stored on your server.

This is not an official Kroger product. Questions about this policy: this website. The terms of use are at /terms.
